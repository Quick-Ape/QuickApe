// security

Security, in plain terms.

A trading extension asks for a lot of trust. Here is exactly what QuickApe does with your keys and clicks, and the code is open source on GitHub so you can check it.

Non-custodial

No accounts, no database. Keys never leave your browser.

Encrypted vault

Your password unlocks an AES-256 vault. Nothing is stored in plain text.

Locks itself

The unlock key clears when the browser closes and auto-locks after you go idle.

Pages can't press buy

Buttons live in a closed shadow DOM and only respond to real clicks.

Your limits

Max buy per chain, double-click protection, and a review screen on every withdrawal.

Password stays home

You only type it in the extension popup, never into a website.

// faq

Questions, answered.

What does QuickApe charge?

0.5% of each buy and sell, about half the 1% most terminals charge. It's paid in SOL, ETH, or BNB inside the same swap, shown in the panel before you click, and visible on the explorer. Network, priority, and DEX fees are separate and apply on every platform.

Is there a discount?

A holder discount to 0.3% is planned for the QuickApe token launch. Until then everyone pays the same 0.5%.

Do I have to import my main wallet?

No, and you shouldn't. Create a trading wallet and fund it with what you plan to trade, or use connected mode so Phantom or MetaMask approves every trade.

Where are my keys stored?

Encrypted with your password inside your browser. QuickApe has no accounts or database, so no server holds your keys or trade history.

Which chains and sites are supported?

Solana, Base, BSC, and Ethereum. Quick-buy works on X, GMGN, Dexscreener, pump.fun, Fomo, Birdeye, Axiom, and Telegram Web.

Can I buy tokens still on the pump.fun bonding curve?

Yes. Solana trades route through Jupiter, which supports the pump.fun curve, Meteora DBC, Raydium LaunchLab, and other launchpads.

What if a token is a honeypot?

QuickApe flags honeypots, high tax, freeze or mint authority, and thin liquidity before you click. Flags help you decide; they can't guarantee a token is safe.

Which browsers work?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers, including Brave, Edge, and Arc.

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely volatile and most go to zero. Only trade what you can afford to lose.
