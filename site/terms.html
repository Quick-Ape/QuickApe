1. What QuickApe is

QuickApe is a browser extension that helps you build and sign your own cryptocurrency swap transactions from websites such as X, GMGN, Dexscreener, pump.fun, and Fomo. QuickApe is non-custodial software. It does not hold your funds, operate an exchange, or act as a broker.

2. Your wallet and keys

You are responsible for your trading wallet, its seed phrase, and your password. We cannot recover a lost password or seed phrase. Use a dedicated trading wallet that only holds funds you are prepared to trade. In connected mode, your own wallet software signs every transaction.

3. Fees

QuickApe charges a fee of 0.5% of each buy and sell, unless a lower rate is shown to you before you trade. The fee is included in the swap transaction, paid in the network's native coin, and shown in the trading panel before you confirm. Network fees, priority fees or tips, and the fees of the underlying exchanges are separate and are not paid to QuickApe.

4. Risks

  • Memecoins and other tokens are highly speculative. Most lose most of their value, and you can lose everything you trade.
  • Rug pulls, honeypots, high taxes, and manipulated launches are common. QuickApe's safety flags are informational and do not guarantee that a token is safe.
  • Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Check amounts, slippage, and destination addresses before you confirm.
  • One-click trading makes impulsive trades easy. Set a small maximum buy.
